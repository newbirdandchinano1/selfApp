Weather atmosphere looping video clips
======================================

Primary runtime format: MP4 (*.mp4) via expo-video
Legacy animated WebP (*.webp) may remain on disk but are not referenced by the app.

Clips:
  sunny.mp4
  cloudy.mp4
  overcast.mp4
  fog.mp4
  rain-light.mp4
  rain.mp4
  snow.mp4
  thunderstorm.mp4

Replace any *.mp4 with the same filename to swap visuals.

Keep clips short, muted, and loop-friendly (seamless loop preferred).
Recommended encode (example):
  ffmpeg -y -i SRC.mp4 -an -vf "fps=24,scale=720:-2" -c:v libx264 -pix_fmt yuv420p -movflags +faststart NAME.mp4
